# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and
do not harass, insult or publish anyone's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Capadonna-Labs/DuckClaw/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message to a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainers may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following
the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
